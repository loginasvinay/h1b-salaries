Methodology

Denial rate = denied ÷ (certified + certified-expired + denied) decisions. Small-sample rates are excluded via minimum-decision thresholds. Denials are often technical — recruitment paperwork defects, prevailing-wage issues, or audit findings — not necessarily a reflection of the worker or the job. Rates are estimates from public DOL records as published, which may contain errors.
