Source: U.S. Department of State, November 2026 Visa Bulletin (released Oct 22, 2026).

How to read it

Final Action Dates

The date your green-card number becomes available. If your priority date (when your petition was filed) is earlier than the date in this chart for your category and country, USCIS or the consulate can approve your case.

Dates for Filing

The earliest date you can submit your adjustment-of-status application or start document collection with the National Visa Center — a head start before your Final Action Date is current.
