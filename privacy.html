Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

1. What we collect: nothing

This site does not collect, store, or process any personal information. There are no user accounts, no contact forms, no newsletters, and no analytics running on this site. Your searches and page views stay in your browser — we never see them.

2. Cookies

This site does not set any cookies of its own. If advertising is introduced in the future (e.g., Google AdSense), ad providers may use cookies to serve and measure ads, as described in their own privacy policies. We will update this section if that changes.

3. California and U.S. state privacy laws

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and similar state privacy laws, you have rights regarding your personal information — including the right to know, delete, and opt out of its sale or sharing. Because this site collects no personal information, there is nothing to sell, share, or delete, and these rights require no action on your part. If our practices change, we will update this policy and provide the required opt-out mechanisms.

4. GDPR (visitors from the EU/UK)

We process no personal data, so there is no data controller activity to consent to or object to. If advertising or analytics are added later, this policy will be updated with the lawful basis, retention details, and your rights.

5. Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience reference and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

6. Data security

With no user data collected, there is no user data at risk on our systems. The site is served as static files over HTTPS once deployed.

7. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our data practices change. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, no contact email is published on this site yet. When one is available, it will be published on the about page.
