Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

1. What we collect

This site has no user accounts, contact forms or newsletters, and we do not ask you for personal information. Your searches on this site run in your browser and are not sent to us.

The site shows advertising from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may collect information about your visit, such as your IP address, browser and device details, and the pages you view, to serve and measure ads. That collection is done by Google, not by us, under Google's own policies.

2. Cookies and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google in Google Ads Settings, and from many other third-party vendors at aboutads.info/choices. To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The site itself sets no cookies of its own. It stores one setting in your browser (local storage) to remember that you dismissed the disclaimer banner; this never leaves your device.

3. Visitors in the EU, UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before cookies are used for personalized ads, through Google's consent message. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time through the privacy settings link shown by that message. Without consent, Google may show non-personalized or limited ads.

4. California and other U.S. state privacy laws

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and similar state laws, you have rights to know about, delete, and opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. We do not sell personal information. Advertising cookies set by Google may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising; you can opt out using Google Ads Settings above, and browsers that send a Global Privacy Control signal are treated as an opt-out by Google where supported.

5. Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience reference and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

6. Data security

The site is served as static files over HTTPS. We do not store visitor data on our systems.

7. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our data practices change, for example if we add analytics. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@clearinfohub.com.
